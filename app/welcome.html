What Peasmine is, and isn’t.

One app, not tenPeriod, mood, meals, spending, to-dos: keep only the ones you use, all in the same place.
Tools that talk to each otherEverything you log lands in one personal database, sorted by day. A meal, a mood and a purchase from the same afternoon sit side by side.
Small is enoughA line, a tap, a half-thought. Jot things down as they happen. No streaks, no scores, no one watching.
Private by designYour records stay on your device. No account needed, no ads, no tracking.

Good to know

  • Make Home yours. Press and hold any tile, or tap Edit home. Drag to reorder, resize, or put away what you don’t use. Put-away tools keep their records and can come back from Add a tool.
  • Need something else? Tap Add a tool and say it in a sentence, like “how much water I drink”. Peasmine makes a small tracker for it.
  • Let the diary do the sorting. Write the way you think. When you save, Peasmine shows where each bit would go, and nothing is filed until you confirm.
  • Make it feel like you. 6 colour themes, 4 icon styles (Doodle, Pixel, Line, Sticker) and 8 languages, all in Settings.
  • Keep a copy. Because your data lives only on your device, export a backup from Settings now and then, and before switching devices.
